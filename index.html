hey, i'm ahnaf,

i really like a good conversation. the kind that stretches much longer than either person expected, usually with someone i didn't know very well before it started.

i've had them with random strangers on buses, people in parks, and people in countries halfway across the world, sometimes when we barely speak the same language.

i think all of those conversations have given me a pretty deep sense of optimism about the world. people are endlessly interesting. everyone seems to have something they care about, something they're curious about, or some way of seeing the world that i never would have arrived at on my own.

the world feels really big to me because of that. full of people i haven't met yet, things i don't understand yet, and ideas i haven't even thought to ask about.

i also dream really big. i think a lot of that comes from growing up around people who always made me feel like i was allowed to.

right now, i'm trying to become a deeper and more deliberate thinker. to question my first instincts, sit with ideas for longer, and push them further than where they naturally stop. i'm reading a lot more as part of that.

i'm also trying to take my health and fitness more seriously, and to love myself as aggressively as possible.

everything i do in life is to have more interesting conversations.
